# Wyte AI pricing and credit economics

## Free
- 5 generations per day
- Wyte Auto
- Standard quality
- Basic prompt enhancement
- Suggestions, basic templates, gallery/history

## Pro
- ₦10,000 / $10 monthly
- 500 creative credits
- All 5 models
- HD, advanced editing, image-to-image/reference images
- Batch generation
- Campaign Mode
- Brand Memory
- Character consistency
- Pro templates
- Advanced prompt engine
- Priority processing
- Extended history

## Credit policy
- Standard: 1
- Premium: 2
- HD: 2
- Advanced edit: 2
- Character: 2
- Campaign asset: 1
- Batch of 4: 4

Do not promise 500 images. Pro receives 500 credits so model/operation costs can be controlled.

## Launch economics
Before increasing free limits or adding expensive models, measure:
1. average credits consumed per Pro user
2. AI provider cost per credit
3. payment processing cost
4. refunds/chargebacks
5. gross margin per Pro subscriber

Target a sustainable blended AI cost per paying user rather than optimizing only for maximum generation volume.
